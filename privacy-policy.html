Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

Introduction

Soil Tester ("the App") is a standalone Android application for collecting soil sensor data via USB-C connected sensors. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

Data Collection

The App collects and stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Soil sensor readings — moisture, temperature, pH, electrical conductivity, nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium values from your connected USB-C sensor.
  • GPS location — latitude and longitude of where each reading is taken, used to display readings on a map. Location access requires your explicit permission.
  • Photos — soil photos you choose to take and attach to readings. Camera access requires your explicit permission.
  • Labels and soil type — text labels and soil classifications you assign to readings.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using an on-device database. The App does not transmit any data to external servers, cloud services, or third parties. There is no account creation, login, or online synchronization.

Data Sharing

The App does not share your data with any third parties. You may choose to export your data as CSV, PDF, or JSON backup files using Android's share feature. When you do so, the data is shared only with the app you choose (e.g., Gmail, Google Drive) and is subject to that app's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • USB Host — to communicate with the soil sensor via USB-C.
  • Location (optional) — to tag readings with GPS coordinates. You can deny this permission and still use the App.
  • Camera (optional) — to take soil photos. You can deny this permission and still use the App.
  • Notifications — to notify you during batch sampling mode.

Third-Party Services

The App uses OpenStreetMap (via osmdroid) to display map tiles. When viewing the Map tab, map tile images are downloaded from OpenStreetMap servers. No personal data is sent with these requests. OpenStreetMap's privacy policy applies to these tile requests.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual readings by swiping in the History tab, or delete all readings from Settings. Uninstalling the App removes all stored data from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
